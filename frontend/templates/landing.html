Question
What is Dense Passage Retrieval (DPR) and how does it work?
AI-generated answer

Dense Passage Retrieval (DPR) is an AI search method that understands the meaning behind your question instead of looking only for exact keyword matches.

It converts both your query and sections of your documents into semantic coordinates. This allows DocMind to find the most relevant passage in milliseconds, even when your question uses completely different words, and deliver an accurate answer grounded directly in the text.
